What do I install?

Install Build on the computer that runs your agents, then connect from your other screens. Mobile devices connect to that host; they are not offered a desktop binary.

Choose a host build
Which agents can I use?

Build works with coding harnesses already installed and authenticated on your host. Claude Code, Codex, and Pi are available now; OpenCode is planned.

See the maintained integration list
What does Build store?

Build stores your account, device registration, content-free connection diagnostics, and optional browser push subscription. Agents execute on your personal hosts.
